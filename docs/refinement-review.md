Use the installed Impeccable 4.1.1 skill to refine the existing cinematic forest-green, paper and serif identity. The owner explicitly chose equal attention for asset owners and partners. This is not a redesign, a new investment offering or a production release.

## What was too much

- The engine diagram, four-question checklist and financing lifecycle repeated related explanations at equal visual weight. Keep the diagram and lifecycle visible; make the checklist available on demand.
- The comparison repeated the same two photos and then repeated its evidence in prose and tabular form. Show each scene once, retain the concise prose, and disclose the detailed comparison when requested.
- Each financing step repeated its paragraph in a separate actor/evidence panel. Keep the four-step reading path and consolidate supporting records into one labelled disclosure.
- Stored-grain facts repeated asset, evidence, rights and participant concepts already introduced above. Keep its specific introduction and qualification visible, with documentation details directly below.
- The diagram's looping light and grid were decorative rather than informative. Remove them and let the two landscape photos carry restrained depth through scroll-linked motion.

Native disclosures retain keyboard and no-JavaScript access. Investor rights, launch-stage status, risk qualifications, the AIFC partnership and company/regulatory records are not hidden or rewritten to imply approval.

## What still needs approved source material

- Named team members, their actual roles and approved biographies/photos: accountability is more useful than additional generic platform prose.

- Instrument-specific eligibility, costs and documents only when approved and actually available. Do not invent rates, returns, commitments, liquidity, service levels or guarantees to fill the page.

These are editorial recommendations, not newly asserted company facts. They were deliberately not fabricated or published as claims.
